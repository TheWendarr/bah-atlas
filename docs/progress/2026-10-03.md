The remaining Layer 1 nodes needed their current download URLs verified, and three hosts reject scripted downloads (DTMO returns 403, FRED drops the connection, HUD returns an empty response to partial requests), so their acquisition paths had to be reworked. **M2 closed on its due date.**

- **Reporting moved into the repository.** Per instructor feedback, the weekly Word report (WAR) is retired. Milestones, node status, and progress now live in [`docs/WBS.md`](../WBS.md), generated from [`docs/wbs/nodes.csv`](../wbs/nodes.csv), with this log carrying the weekly narrative. The WAR's colour key (black / green / purple / red) carries over unchanged.
- **Source decisions.** A5 uses HUD's *revised* FY2026 SAFMRs (effective 21 May 2026) rather than the original October 2025 release. A6 publishes rent and utilities as rounded percentages of each MHA's BAH, so the utilities adjustment carries roughly ±0.5 percentage points of precision. A1 and A6 are browser downloads because travel.dod.mil blocks scripts; `make fetch` validates and checksums both.
- **A7 source switched.** Mortgage rates now come straight from Freddie Mac's `PMMS_history.csv` instead of the FRED mirror, which drops scripted downloads. Freddie Mac is the original publisher, so the citation also improves.
- **Profiles (A12).** Primary: E-5 with dependents (DoD standard: 2-bedroom townhouse). Contrast: O-3 with dependents (3-bedroom single-family), which differs from the primary in grade band, bedroom count, and dwelling type.
- **Classification (A13).** Coverage-ratio classes are fixed breaks centred on the 95% statutory target rather than 1.00 as drafted in the nodal architecture, because the research question tests the 95% target. Fixed breaks keep maps comparable across profiles.
