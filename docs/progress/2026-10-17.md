| B12–B15 | Decision | A flat 0.95 target mis-scores BAH's own formula. 37 U.S.C. 403(b)(3) subtracts the same dollar cost share (5% of the *national* average cost) at every location, so BAH equals exactly 95% of local cost only where local cost equals the national mean. Cheap MHAs score short and expensive MHAs score over by design, and because cost levels cluster, the flat-95% gap would carry spatial autocorrelation into the 2.6 tests by construction. | Changed the test variable to **adequacy_gap** = (BAH + cost share) ÷ local cost − 1, target 1.00, using DTMO's 2026 out-of-pocket amounts (E-5 w/ deps $126, O-3 w/ deps $151). The flat-95% gap is kept as a sensitivity column, and the results file lists which MHAs change status between the two readings. Commit [`c649838`](https://github.com/TheWendarr/bah-atlas/commit/c649838498801fc54693c77439b3f6cdfeb83f6a). | **Yes:** the [research proposal](../research-proposal.md) states the test variable as Gap = R − 0.95. Do you approve adequacy_gap as the primary 2.6 test variable, with the flat-95% gap reported as a sensitivity check? |
| B8 | Decision | Three Hamptons ZIPs (11930, 11963, 11976) report real summer asking rents of $50k–$100k+ a month in the 2026 ZORI release, which stopped the B8 build at its $50k ceiling. | Raised the hard ceiling to $200k (still catches a home-value file passed as rent) and list any ZIP above $25k in the B8 manifest as a warning, with its MHA.
