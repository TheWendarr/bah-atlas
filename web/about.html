H2: what is associated with the adequacy gap?

  • Part of the negative ln_rent coefficient is mechanical: adequacy_gap divides by utilities-inclusive ZORI, and ln_rent is the log of ZORI, so rent sits on both sides of the model. B23 QA report, section 11
  • 201 of 283 eligible MHAs have a rent-growth series and enter the model. The 82 without one differ from those modelled: their median adequacy gap is 0.0543 against 0.1563, and they are less dense. B23 QA report, section 11
